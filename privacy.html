Privacy and capture limits

Captcher Recorder is operated by Noventix LLC. You choose what to record and when to submit it. Use synthetic data, and review the resulting draft before sharing.

What is recorded

Starting a recording saves HTML snapshots and interactions from the starting tab and tabs opened from that flow. Snapshots can contain visible and hidden page content, ordinary form values, URLs, titles, images, styles and fonts. Eligible pages also include a viewport screenshot. Single-page capture saves the page you select. Recording stops on Finish, Discard, panel closure or loss of its connection.

Outside a recording, the panel inspects active-tab URLs to support pairing and checks recognized Captcher origins. Presence scripts announce the installed extension on the connected service. It does not run a general browsing-history or analytics collection service.

What redaction can and cannot do

Recognized password fields (including ones revealed with a show-password button), password/one-time-code/card autocomplete controls, fields named like passwords, PINs or card codes, and hidden inputs are blanked during capture. This includes sensitive dropdowns, templates, open and closed shadow roots, and accessible same-origin frames. Form values are restored afterwards. Sensitive actions use placeholders. Screenshots are omitted if recognized sensitive controls or embedded frames are present.

Capture stops if protection cannot be verified, including inaccessible or loading frames and embedded objects. Common encoded reflections are checked; ambiguous short values can cause capture to stop. Redaction does not identify every secret in ordinary text, URLs, images, canvases, custom encodings or unmarked fields. Personal, authentication, financial, health, location or communications data may therefore be included if it appears in what you record.

Where data goes

Submit sends the capture to the connected Captcher server shown in the panel. The extension pairs only with the Captcher app at app.captcher.app, over HTTPS. Local development servers are accepted only by unpacked development builds, never by the store version. The hosted service processes recordings to generate walkthroughs, including sending content to Anthropic. Its hosting, account handling, retention and subprocessors are described in the service privacy notice. The extension contains no advertising or analytics SDK.

To embed page resources, the capture engine requests URLs named by the page using the page's browser-enforced access rules. Its fetch adapter omits credentials, rejects redirects and limits each response to 32 MB. There is no privileged cross-origin resource proxy. Resource servers can receive ordinary connection metadata, including your IP address and requested URL. Missing or blocked resources can reduce snapshot fidelity.

Local storage and control

When it pairs, the extension sends the Captcher app a device label (browser name, operating system and recorder version) so you can recognize the connection in your account. The extension stores its service address, device token, connected email and preferences in local extension storage. Content scripts cannot read that storage. Captures and logs stay in panel memory until submission, download or closure. Closing the panel loses unsent work. Downloads remain wherever you save them; uninstalling the extension does not delete downloads or recordings already submitted to the service.

Use Reconnect or change account in Settings to replace the connection. Revoking the device on the service prevents future authenticated uploads. If an upload is rejected, reconnect and retry while keeping the panel open. Removing the extension clears its extension storage.
